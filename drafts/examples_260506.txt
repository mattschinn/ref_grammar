beoo` /bɛo'o/, from General American "(a) bit ago" /bɪɾəgoʊ/. The tap is elided out. The vowel /ɪ/ lowers to /ɛ/ while the schwa smoothes with stress-carrying /o/. Means "earlier".

réśrè, /ɾɛs.'ɾɛ/, means "Yesterday". from GA "yesterday" /jɛs·tɻ·ɾɛɪ/. "Yesterday" is a common word and the syllabic rhotic simplifies to schwa, in everyday colloquial speech. In dialect, the schwa is dropped entirely. In colloquial GA, the /d/ is already tapped in rapid speech. This is formalized in the dialectal descendant. For dialect-specific changes, the /j/ shifts to /ɾ/ while /st/ cluster simplifies to /s/.
