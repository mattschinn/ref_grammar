### nocquıî

#### Etymology
*not quite* — adverbial phrase "approximately; falling short of," colloquial GA /nɑʔ kwaɪʔ/ (from /nɑt kwaɪt/ with coda glottalization). Lexicalized as a single unit before grammaticalizing as the non-volitive-negative marker. The approximative-from-below semantics is inherited and underlies both the high-scope frustrative reading and the low-scope attenuative reading. Also productive as a derivational element forming "a small lack" compounds (see Morphology).

**Part of speech:** gram. (LVC.NONVOL.NEG); also low-scope attenuative; also adv. (temporal proximative, substitutional apologetic); also deriv. prefix.  **IPA:** /noˈkʷːi/  **Foot:** [Open].

Phonological development: /ɑ/ → /ɔ/ → /o/ (raising-and-rounding; triggered word-initially and after /n/, /h/, /k/ — see phonology notes); /aɪ/ → /i/; /ʔk/ → geminate /kʷː/ (the coda glottal stop of *not* assimilates to and geminates the following /k/, which labializes from the /w/ of *quite*); final coda /ʔ/ (from *quite*'s /t/) drops, but can trigger gemination of a following word-initial consonant in compounds (see Morphology). The final /i/ from /aɪ/ carries stress and pitch (circumflex in headword).

Cross-refs: *skwkw* (paradigmatic opposite on the polarity axis, volitive side); *rhiiy* (concord reinforcer in the LVC, **and** the general negator); *ęy* (parallel reinforcer in the *skwkw* paradigm). Also the non-volitive-positive marker (GET-ONESELF, *to be entered*) and volitive-positive marker (GO-AHEAD, *to be entered*).

#### Definition

1. *(gram., LVC.NONVOL.NEG)* the non-volitive-negative cell of the light verb complex; marks falling-short, typically with a frustrative reading (a trajectory toward V, often involuntary, that fails to reach V, with the not-reaching foregrounded and often regretful). — *I do nocquıî get myself tuning them out.* "I couldn't manage to tune them out and ended up watching anyway."
2. *(low-scope attenuative)* under a volitive LVC, modifies the lexical verb: "only partway; only a little." — *I go ahead and nocquıî tune them out.* "I deliberately tune them out only a little bit."
3. *(adv., temporal proximative)* before; not yet at. — *on my way to the gym when it's nocquıî Thursday* "…before Thursday."
4. *(adv., substitutional apologetic)* rather than; instead of — softened, with an unintentional or mildly apologetic flavor (contrast the deliberate substitutional *skwkw* sense 2). — *on my way to the bar nocquıî the gym* "…the bar, rather than the gym."

#### Morphology

**Derivational prefix *nocquii-***, forming compounds that denote "a small lack" — a quantitative or qualitative falling-short of the base noun. Productive. The combining form is *nocquii-*; the junction behaves per the phonology:

- Before an obstruent, the dropped coda /ʔ/ geminates the following consonant: *nocquii-* + *sprıĩ* "spring" → **nocquiissprıĩ** (with geminate /sː/). When the geminating consonant is /k/, gemination **bleeds debuccalization** (a geminate /kː/ does not debuccalize to /h/ the way a singleton onset /k/ does): *nocquii-* + (coffee) → **nocquiikkoþii**, where the base surfaces with protected /kː/ even though the free word for "coffee" shows /h/ (*hoþii*).
- Before a vowel or rhotic, a linker *-e-* appears: *nocquii-* + *ŕobsǫ* "chromosome" → **nocquiie-ŕobsǫ**.

[Open: the combining-form rule above is inferred from the three attested compounds; ratify. Open: stress/pitch placement in the compounds — whether stress stays on *nocquii*'s final /i/ or shifts to the base — undetermined; compound IPA and circumflex placement left [Open] below.]

#### Notes

Pairs with the reinforcer *rhiiy* (sense 1): an optional emphatic concord particle sitting clause-finally, licensed only by *nocquıî* within the LVC and not surviving the adverbial uses (senses 3–4). — *I'm not quite getting myself to pass out rhiiy.* "I'm falling short of really/fully passing out."

The frustrative reading (sense 1) is compositional — falling-short semantics + the non-volitive frame + the action's natural endpoint — not separately encoded. *rhiiy* emphasizes the falling-short but does not produce the flavor. [Open: predicted sharper frustrative with telic predicates than atelic; untested.]

Has both high scope (sense 1) and low scope (sense 2), in contrast to *skwkw* (high-scope only). The split follows from the degree-of-approximation core, which can target a whole event or the realization-level within an event.

### ęy

#### Etymology
*any* — NPI determiner/pronoun "any whatsoever," GA /eni/. Inherited as a strict negative-polarity item, narrowed to the concord reinforcer of the volitive-negative marker *skwkw*.

**Part of speech:** gram. (concord particle / NPI, distributionally restricted).  **IPA:** /ẽj̃/  **Foot:** [Open].

Phonological development: intervocalic /n/ elides, leaving nasalization on the flanking vowels (E1); the resulting two syllables smooth to a single nasalized /ej/ diphthong — smoothing here is conditioned by function-word status (a lexical word such as *penny* would remain disyllabic; see phonology notes).

Cross-refs: *skwkw* (sole licensor); *rhiiy* (parallel reinforcer, in the *nocquıî* paradigm).

#### Definition

1. *(gram., VOL.NEG concord reinforcer)* emphatic floor-of-the-scale particle, sitting with the action or object quantified over; licensed only by *skwkw* within the LVC. Reinforces categorical refusal: "any instance whatsoever." — *I'm skwkw passing out ęy.* "I'm refusing to fall asleep at all."

#### Notes

Doubly restricted: licensed only in negative contexts (NPI behavior) and, within those, only by verbal *skwkw*. Does not survive the adverbial/substitutional uses of *skwkw* (*on my way to the bar skwkw ęy gym* is unnatural). The concord is paradigm-internal, not a property of *skwkw* wherever it appears.

In nested negation, *ęy* sits locally with the lexical verb (post-*skwkw*), while the outer marker's reinforcer (*rhiiy*, for *nocquıî*) sits clause-finally — the two reinforcers occupy different structural positions and do not compete. — *nocquıî go ahead and skwkw ęy V rhiiy.*

[Open: *ęy* may have other NPI distributions (questions, conditionals, comparatives) not yet described.]

### rhiiy

A polyfunctional morpheme from GA *really*, spanning intensifier prefix → bleached lexical prefix → non-volitive-negative reinforcer → general negator. The bound-prefix uses appear inside *rhiiynii*, *rhiiysseunnou*, and *rhiiyepplii*; the free-word uses (reinforcer, general negator) are described here. Consolidated as a single lemma per the 2026-05-19 decision: same form /ɻiː/, same primary source.

**Part of speech:** gram. (general negation); also gram. (NONVOL.NEG concord reinforcer); also deriv./intensifier prefix `rhiiy-`.  **IPA:** /ɻiː/  **Foot:** [Open].

Phonological development: GA /ˈɹɪl.i/ → /ɻiː/: dark velarized /ɫ/, sandwiched between front vowels, becomes /j/ and then elides, leaving a lengthened /iː/; onset /ɹ/ → /ɻ/. (Same derivation as the `rhiiy-` prefix attested in *rhiiynii*, *rhiiysseunnou*, *rhiiyepplii* — this entry consolidates that morpheme.)

Cross-refs: *nocquıî* (host for the reinforcer reading, sense 2); *skwkw* (volitive-domain negator — complementary distribution by domain); *ęy* (parallel reinforcer, in the *skwkw* paradigm); *rhiiynii*, *rhiiysseunnou*, *rhiiyepplii* (contain the bound-prefix uses, senses 3–4).

#### Definition

1. *(gram., general negation)* "no"; "not so." Volition-neutral propositional negation, used outside the LVC: phrasal denial, short answers, negation of non-verbal predicates. — *Rhiiy.* "No." / "Not so."
   - Etymology of this sense: *not really* (colloquial GA general negator), grammaticalized via a Jespersen-cycle pathway — the *not* erodes, leaving bare *really* to carry negative force on its own (compare French *personne*). This sense's source phrase is *not really*, distinct from the bare-*really* source of senses 2–4; the forms converge on /ɻiː/.
2. *(gram., NONVOL.NEG concord reinforcer)* clause-final emphatic concord with *nocquıî* in the LVC; emphasizes the predicate's full realization, the standard the action falls short of. — *I'm not quite getting myself to pass out rhiiy.*
   - Etymology of this sense: GA intensifier *really* "to a real degree."
3. *(intensifier prefix `rhiiy-`)* still-transparent intensifier on a verb stem. — attested in *rhiiyepplii* "to apply oneself with real effort" (under *epplii* sense 2).
   - Etymology: GA intensifier *really*.
4. *(bleached lexical prefix `rhiiy-`)* fully bleached, no longer felt as an intensifier; part of a lexicalized stem. — attested in *rhiiynii* "to need/want" and *rhiiysseunnou* "to be different/excellent."
   - Etymology: GA *really*, bleached.

#### Notes

Senses 1 and 2 are **the same morpheme, contextually disambiguated**, not homophones: inside the LVC with *nocquıî* upstream → reinforcer reading (sense 2); outside the LVC, or without *nocquıî* → negator reading (sense 1). The two play off a shared "realness" core — the reinforcer asserts the predicate's realness as the standard fallen short of; the negator denies the predicate's realness outright.

Senses 3–4 are the bound-prefix uses, which show grammaticalization stratification across the lexicon: transparent in *rhiiyepplii* (sense 3), fully bleached in *rhiiynii* and *rhiiysseunnou* (sense 4).

Does not enter the LVC as a polarity marker (only as the sense-2 reinforcer); verbal negation is handled by the dedicated volition-aware markers *skwkw* and *nocquıî*. A weakened-volitional LVC use was considered and rejected (it would erode the volitive/non-volitive binary).

Fills the standalone declarative-negation ("no") gap: *skwkw* standalone is prohibitive (a command), *nocquıî* has no standalone use, so *rhiiy* is the language's assertional "no."

[Open: whether negator-*rhiiy* itself attracts a new reinforcer over time (a Jespersen spiral). Deferred — long-horizon.]

### skwkw

#### Etymology
*skip* — GA /skɪp/, "decline; pass over." Lexicalized as the volitive-negative marker. The deliberate-declination semantics is inherited and sharpened to active counter-volition (going out of one's way to not-do, not merely the absence of volition).

**Part of speech:** gram. (LVC.VOL.NEG); also adv./substitutional; also interj. (prohibitive).  **IPA:** /skʷkʷ/ (first /kʷ/ syllabic).  **Foot:** [omitted].

Phonological development: irregular, peculiar to this high-frequency form. Coda /p/ delabializes — but only after labializing the /k/ (the labial feature transfers from /p/ to /k/ before /p/ is lost). The vowel /ɪ/, already central and squeezed between two relatively resonant /kʷ/ sequences, elides entirely, leaving a vowelless /skʷkʷ/ with the first /kʷ/ as syllable nucleus.

Cross-refs: *nocquıî* (paradigmatic opposite on the polarity axis, non-volitive side); *rhiiy* (general negator, complementary domain — outside the LVC); *ęy* (concord reinforcer, licensed only by this marker). Also the volitive-positive marker (GO-AHEAD, *to be entered*).

#### Definition

1. *(gram., LVC.VOL.NEG)* the volitive-negative cell of the light verb complex; marks deliberate refusal or active counter-volition. High-scope only (operates on the predication, never as a lexical-verb modifier). — *I'm skwkw passing out ęy.* "I'm deliberately refusing to fall asleep (not even a little)."
2. *(adv./substitutional)* in place of, instead of, declining the alternative; deliberate, with a mutual-exclusivity flavor. — *on my way to the bar skwkw the gym* "…the bar, deliberately not the gym."
3. *(interj., prohibitive)* "Don't!"; "Pass on it!" — standalone negative imperative. — *Skwkw!* "Don't!"

#### Notes

A showcase for vowelless syllabification: /skʷkʷ/ has no vocalic nucleus, the first /kʷ/ serving as the syllable's nucleus. Novel for the lexicon (other consonant-heavy forms — *hoʔnʔnts*, *ritsstw*, *toutw* — retain at least one full vowel). [Open: foot structure and the metrical status of a syllabic obstruent.]

Pairs with the reinforcer *ęy* (sense 1 only): an optional emphatic floor-of-the-scale particle, licensed only by *skwkw* within the LVC, not surviving the adverbial/substitutional uses (sense 2). See *ęy*.

Does not stack with GO-AHEAD: *going ahead and skwkw V* collapses to the *skwkw* reading (the volitive-positive marker contributes only rhetorical emphasis, no second feature value), since both fill the single volition slot.

Layers with the non-volitive-positive marker (GET-ONESELF) productively but narrowly: *getting myself to skwkw V* = "finding myself in the position of having to deliberately refuse V" (the refusal is deliberate; arriving at it is not). Felicitous only in forced-choice contexts.

## Proposed collation positions (for §4 insertion)

All four marker headwords now have derived forms and can be collated per §2:

- **ęy** /ẽj̃/ — ę-region (after the e-region; order is `e, ę, æ`). No existing e-/ę- entries, so first of its region.
- **nocquıî** /noˈkʷːi/ — n-region, after *nobêt* (`nob…` < `nocq…`). The three *nocquii-* compounds (**nocquiie-ŕobsǫ**, **nocquiikkoþii**, **nocquiissprıĩ**) sort after *nocquıî*; among themselves, by §2 Latin-order-on-subsequent-characters: `nocquiie-` < `nocquiik-` < `nocquiis-` (`e < k < s`).
- **rhiiy** /ɻiː/ — rh-region, before *rhiiynii* and *rhiiysseunnou* (it is a proper prefix of both, so sorts first).
- **skwkw** /skʷkʷ/ — s-region (after the rhotic region `…ŕ, s…`). No existing s- entries, so first of its region.

## Phonology-update notes (route to `phonology.md` in a dedicated session — not applied here)

1. **/ɑ/ → /ɔ/ → /o/ raising-and-rounding.** Triggered word-initially (no preceding consonant), and after /n/, /h/, /k/. The /h/ and /k/ environments are historically one place of articulation (/h/ being debuccalized /k/), so they align naturally. The /k/ environment is rare in surface terms because /k/ usually debuccalizes to /h/; it surfaces in geminate-protected environments (e.g., *nocquiikkoþii*). Possibly other consonants trigger this too — to investigate. Attested: *nocquıî*, *nocquiikkoþii*.
2. **/ʔk/ → geminate /kʷː/, and coda-/ʔ/ junction gemination.** A coda glottal stop assimilates to and geminates a following /k/ (labializing it from an adjacent /w/). More generally, a dropped coda /ʔ/ can trigger gemination of a following word-initial consonant across a compound junction. Attested: *nocquıî* internally; *nocquiissprıĩ*, *nocquiikkoþii* at junctions.
3. **Gemination counterbleeds debuccalization.** A geminate /kː/ does not undergo /k/ → /h/ debuccalization (which targets singleton onsets). Junction-gemination therefore protects a base /k/ that would otherwise lenite — yielding /h/ ~ /kː/ alternations (free *hoþii* vs. bound *-kkoþii*). Attested: *nocquiikkoþii*.
4. **Function-word smoothing vs. lexical-word retention.** Intervocalic /n/-elision-with-nasalization (E1) feeds vowel smoothing in function words (*any* → *ęy* /ẽj̃/, monosyllabic), but lexical words retain the disyllabic outcome (*penny* would stay disyllabic). The smoothing is conditioned by function-word status. Attested: *ęy*; contrast example *penny*.
5. **Vowel elision between resonant /kʷ/ sequences (irregular).** In *skwkw* /skʷkʷ/, GA /skɪp/'s /ɪ/ elides between two /kʷ/, with the coda /p/'s labial feature transferring to the /k/ before /p/ is lost. Flagged as irregular/peculiar to this high-frequency form, not a regular rule.
