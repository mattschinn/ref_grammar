## 2. Decisions from the 2026-06-11 discussion session

### 2.1 REC-ITER demotion **[Settled 2026-06-11; residue spec Provisional pending battery B1]**

**Decision.** The recognitional-iterative nominal construction is **a special case of the general frame-plus-nominal engagement construction** (the possession construction, §2.4). The habitual reading of *the walks* is compositional: REC (shared-knowledge type) + ITER (iteration) + frame (engagement/possession). This retroactively explains the F-column distribution (e.g. *the being* fails for lack of a possessable, type-able nominal).

**Two-feature residue** distinguishing the deverbal special case:
1. **Do-support** on the get-oneself side: *rimmase ię þ rishś* — get-oneself + deverbal REC-nominal requires the mediator *ię* (< GA *doing*), where plain nouns take a bare nominal (*rimmaseu rhink*). Go-ahead uses *with* in both cases. Systematicity to be verified — battery B1, §6.
2. **Distinct REC exponent**: REC = *þ* (pre-consonantal) ~ *y* /i/ (pre-vocalic, inheriting GA allomorphy), versus the merged referential article *o*. ITER = *-ś*.

**This resolves** `verbal-system.md` §7.1's placeholder *[Open: REC and ITER surface forms]*.

**The þ/o doublet** is an etymological doublet of GA *the* with divergent grammaticalization paths and phonological outcomes (referential use → weakened, merged with *a* into *o*; recognitional use → fused onto the nominal, segmentally preserved). Cross-linguistic parallel for the Origin subsection: Latin *ille* → Romance articles and third-person pronouns via divergent reduction. Strong naturalistic-texture point.

**Unchanged:** REC ≠ DEF stays **[Settled]** — a claim about morpheme function, independent of document location.

**Relocation plan:** REC/ITER morphology → `language_reference.md` nominal sections (determiner-typology material moves with it); frame-complementation side merges into the possession construction and `verbal-system.md` §4.1 argument structure; `verbal-system.md` §7 reduces to a pointer + the demotion reasoning. Flagship-innovation framing survives as a *nominal-side* innovation. Preserve the full reasoning (this section) per registry practice; §7.4's open questions migrate as nominal-morphology questions.

### 2.2 Matrix particle class **[class Settled 2026-06-11; per-particle detail Provisional]**

**Term:** *matrix particle*. Registry shorthand: *scaffold particle* (informal). Replaces the working label "adverbial relativizer" (nothing is relativized — retire with reasoning).

**Definition.** A closed-ish class of clause-scope particles grammaticalized from GA matrix clauses containing a finite verb (*it ended up..., I know (that)..., it appears (that)..., what I need is...*). They erect a modal frame the clause sits inside.

**Membership criterion:** the etymon contains a finite verb (→ can bear tense). Members: *enp/enniip* (happenstance-outcome), clausal *no/nii* (knowledge), *rhi·iiniies/rhi·iiniir·ros* (desiderative/weak deontic; reduplicated intensive *rhi·ii·rhii·niies*). Candidate: *piies* (appearance) — **[Open: confirm past form]**. Excluded (no finite verb in etymon, no tense): *meiyi* (< *maybe*), *bauyiis* (< *in my eyes*) — these are plain modal adverbs sharing discourse function but not morphology.

**Morphology.**
- **Tense, not aspect:** a privative **past / non-past** opposition; the non-past member is unmarked and freely combines with habitual and future adverbs ("always end up...", "tomorrow, probably end up..."). Settled by Mat's adverb data 06-11.
- **Person syncretism:** unmarked for person; for *rhi·iiniies*, phonetically motivated (GA *I/you/they/we* → /i, jɪ, ɛj, ɻi/, acoustically merged after /ɻ/ and flanked by /i/). Disambiguation via strong-pronoun *on-X-end* phrases with flexible placement.

**Architecture.** Split-TAM: **aspect lives on the weak pronouns; tense lives on the matrix particles.** Typologically coherent (cf. TAM-on-pronoun systems, e.g. Wolof; peripheral fossilization of older morphology generally). Frame the tense survival as architecture, not anomaly.

**Government.** A per-particle lexical property over the clause's aspect: *enp* forces imperfect (the clause "surrenders" its aspect — conjunct-like, Ojibwe parallel for Origin); *no* imposes no requirement. Implies an independent-vs-governed clause-type distinction new to the grammar. Gather per particle before generalizing. **[Open]**

**Syntax.**
- Ordering: **topic particle > matrix particle > clause**; topic particles precede the verb complex generally. **[Settled 06-11]**
- Stacking: grammatical but clunky; requires pronominal *on-X-end* buffering to keep nested domains parseable ("on my end, know that, on their end, ended up..."). Usage note: speakers avoid deep stacking. **[Provisional]**
- *ea*: compatible, clause-final polarity question as usual ("End up going ahead with the dishes at all?"). **[Settled 06-11]**
- Negation: **[Open — Mat thinking]**.

**Homophony note:** matrix *no* ≠ know-topic-particle *no*; distinct syntax (matrix *no* takes NP or full clause and yields a complete sentence; topic *noho* introduces a clausal topic and expects elaboration). Document the relationship and the *No Báuyà, no Báuyà* stacking example.

**Glossing proposal [Provisional]:** per-particle tags — HAPP (*enp*), KNOW (*no* matrix), DESID (*rhi·iiniies*), APPAR (*piies*); tense as .PST on the marked member.

**Origin subsection material:** Thompson & Mulac (*I think* → epistemic parenthetical); Latin American Spanish *dizque* (< *dice que*) as the exact structural parallel for matrix *no*; French *il faut que* / Spanish *ojalá que* family.

**Destination:** new section in `verbal-system.md` (interacts with frames, aspect, pronouns), pointer from `language_reference.md`; terminology-registry entry first.

### 2.3 Periphery asymmetry **[Provisional — stated confidently 06-11; wants worked examples]**

Left and right dislocation are asymmetrically marked:
- **Left dislocation:** visibility particle on the topic (existing analysis).
- **Right dislocation (antitopic):** visibility particle stripped; tagged with the strong-pronoun ***on-X-end*** phrase; definite coloring retained. ("You know Mark, they went ahead and split" vs "They went ahead and split, Mark, on their end.")

Consequences: the visibility contrast is a **left-periphery-only** phenomenon; the strong pronouns gain a **third documented function** (emphasis, possession, antitopic tagging). Destination: `language_reference.md` information-structure material + `verbal-system.md` §5 (strong pronouns).

### 2.4 The possession construction **[Settled in outline; section to be drafted]**

Frame + nominal:
- **get-oneself + N, perfect aspect** → possession ("have"): *rimmaseu beikw*.
- **get-oneself + N, imperfect aspect** → acquisition in progress ("getting").
- **go-ahead (+ *with*) + N** → deliberate selection ("picking and choosing").
- Lexical alternatives modulate: *ǫıĩ* (own) for mere/neutral having (frame choice tunes intentionality emphasis); *rib* (give) for explicit volition in transfer.
- **Give-extension:** causative of the construction — *get*-slot filled by recipient (*ritm o rhémstwè*, "got-them the book") = "give," volition-neutral by default. Constraint recorded: the causative variant resists topic-marked definite objects (*"nou rhémstwè, got-them-it" ungrammatical*) — *rib* + go-ahead used instead. Needs more data. **[Open]**

Destination: new `verbal-system.md` section adjacent to the frames; absorbs the REC-ITER complementation facts per §2.1.

### 2.5 *oþw* separative satellite **[Provisional]**

Non-volitional separative satellite, < GA *off* (the happenstance-detachment particle of *drift off, doze off, nod off, wander off*). Frame affinity: get-oneself — supplies the befall-able path event that lexically volitional bare stems (colloquial GA *split*) lack under that frame. *doze/drift/nod* all pass per Mat's judgment (06-11). Pending: a couple more verb classes before [Settled]. Dictionary entry as derivational satellite, not a quirk of *split*.

### 2.6 The *nouś/not* doublet — and the suppletion disposition **[Settled 2026-06-11]**

**Decision:** *nouś* (< GA *notice*; ś-stem) and *not* (< GA *note*; n/r-stem alternants *noun-*, *nour-*, *nou·-*) are **two tightly related but separate lexemes**, not one suppletive paradigm. Deciding evidence: stem choice is meaning-conditioned, not frame-conditioned — the converse pairings are grammatical and compelling (volitive *nouś* = deliberately try to notice the unexpected; get-oneself *not* = sense by happenstance, incl. non-visual modalities). Frame affinities (*nouś* ↔ get-oneself, *not* ↔ go-ahead) are documented as **tendencies**, with cross-referenced dictionary entries.

**Terminology registry consequences** (three phenomena disentangled from the working word "suppletion"):
1. **Inherited irregularity / irregular concord** — preserved GA strong-verb forms where the regular paradigm predicts the *-t(t)-* concord infix: *meirtt* (< *made it*) for *meiktt*. New registry entry + a §4.2 "irregular concord" subsection.
2. **Homophonous merger** — already named in `verb-recipe-v2.md` §5.3; promote to a registry entry. (*nouś/not* are *near*-merger doublet, a related but distinct outcome: partial collapse with surviving formal contrast.)
3. **Frame-conditioned satellite** — *oþw* (§2.5). New registry entry.
4. ***Suppletion*** — held in reserve; **not currently attested**. Registry entry records the diachronic prediction: poly-resolving pairs are one reanalysis step from genuine frame-suppletion (if speakers ever treat a pair as one lexeme with frame-selected stems). The *nouś/not* doublet is the nearest case and the one to watch; "suppletion in progress" is available as game-lore speaker variation if wanted.

## 7. Method findings & revisions for round 2

### 7.1 The central methodological result

The grid's cell ontology partially dissolved on contact with data: **happenstance, threshold, inchoative, and background are not cells a verb occupies but compositional products of frame × concord shape × aspect × stem lexical aspect**, with matrix particles as a further scaffolding layer. Evidence: the five-way *split* paradigm; the inchoative↔imperfect tie (two concepts); duration→effort→threshold linkage in COGNITION; bare get-oneself under-delivering happenstance in DOING. This is a *finding*, not a failure — but round 2 needs a redesigned instrument whose prediction columns range over the machinery, not the readings.

### 7.2 Instrument revisions

1. **Closing block per concept** (5 lines: cluster verdict / carve decision / cells confirmed-failed-reframed / surprises / doc destinations) — keeps the freeform narrative, which is where discoveries happened, but makes Phase 4 readable instead of reconstructable.
2. **Pair vetting** before commitment: against GA lexicalization (the dishes problem) and against grammar-occupied territory.
3. **Dedicated AB stimulus set** built from verbs predicted to host it; AB proved exactly as selective as predicted but never got an isolated trial in its home concept.
4. **Prediction columns** restated over frame × concord shape (now ×3: infinitive-, progressive-, passive-shaped) × aspect, conditioned on stem lexical aspect.
5. **Per-concept immediate write-up**: discoveries cascade (the matrix-particle discovery in concept 1 reshaped translations in concepts 3–6); batch translation then batch write-up loses the cascade order.
6. **Round 2 concept**: SAYING / COMMUNICATION, as deferred — now *more* interesting than originally scored, since the matrix-particle class predicts a reportative member (a *dizque*-analogue from, e.g., \**they say (that)* / \**I heard (that)*). Add a deixis make-up batch for MOTION pairs 2–5.
