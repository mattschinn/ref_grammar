## 1. Phase 3 verdicts, reconstructed

As in round 1, the formal five-line closing blocks were not produced in the prescribed form; this section reconstructs them from the narrative. Cell-style marks are not used (the round-2 method ranges over machinery, not reading-cells). Sub-categories worked: **A** (bare utterance), **B** (reportative edge), **C** (quotation), **G** partial (interactive/durative), **E** partial (speech acts), **MOTION** make-up. **D** (manner of speaking) and **F** (channel) were **not translated**. **[Open — carry to round 3]**

### 1.1 A — Bare utterance

**Clustering verdict:** *say* dissolves into a **quotative-verb + obligatory ideophone** frame, not a lexical verb. The headline blurt-absorption prediction **holds** — but the absorber is richer than predicted: the surviving core is *o* (< GA *go*, quotative), and the meaning is carried jointly by the frame and an obligatory ideophone follow-up. *tell* grammar-absorbs as predicted (possession-causative, §1.1 below); *talk/speak* outcome is **inconclusive** (the instrument's own weak diagnostic — Mat flagged it).

**Findings.**
- **Quotative *o* as the core "say" verb.** *O* (< GA *go*) in perfect aspect *rhentt* (< GA *went*) = "said." Intransitive (no object), with an **obligatory ideophone** follow-up. The quotative origin shows transparently in the intransitivity + ideophone obligation. *Beunheumm rhentt, rhémmen·bèm* "I said it (boom-boom-boom)." Confirms the round-2 quotative prediction, and overlaps the C-quotation result (§1.3) — *o* is one verb doing both jobs.
- **Blurt-absorption confirmed.** "It slipped out / I blurted it out" = *Rimmaśe oę, rekriiâ* — bare get-oneself frame on *o* + ideophone. No lexical *blurt/let-slip* verb needed. Settled-dust finding of the PERCEPTION-PH type: GA blurt-class verbs do not survive.
- **Ideophone class (major surprise — §2.5).** Speech acts take an obligatory ideophone characterizing the manner: *rhémmen·bèm* (< *boom boom boom*; bullet-pointed/confident/confrontational argumentation), *rekriiâ* (< *yakitty-yak*; speaking in dialect — gives the GA exonym *Rickety Yak*), *rhitmmtm* (< *nuh-uh*; negation/refusal/disagreement), *bauwouwou* (speaking in General American). Multimodal: several carry conventionalized hand gestures. Ideophones may break phonotactics (non-/rare phonemes; ultimate-syllable stress). **This is a new phenomenon class for the grammar.**
- **Infinitive- vs progressive-shaped concord on one stem.** *Rimmaśeto o, rhémmen·bèm* "I finally said it" — infinitive-shaped = threshold/effortful ("finally," omitted lexically). *Rimmaśe oę rhémmen·bèm, …* "I was saying it when…" — progressive-shaped = caught-mid-speech. Confirms the §4.2 concord-shape reframe on a fresh stem.
- **tell = possession-causative (§1.1).** "I told her the news" = *Beunheun þiltm·ę·aų·et, o riųś* "went ahead and filled them in on it, the news." Verb **þilnn** (< GA *fill in*), DO infixed / IO suffixed (-*on*); agglutinated object string *þiltm·ę·ą·et*. Confirms the *ritm* give-extension pattern with an **abstract possessum**, and the definite-object restriction recurs (§3).
- **talk/speak inconclusive.** "We talked for hours" = *Rhi riaśeto rekriiâ þri·ei·iiǫ* (informal chatter, get-oneself = loss of control over time); "I spoke with the manager" = *Beunheun ospokwþ o bäeĩyi* (volitive, deliberate). Mat judged the instrument's talk/speak contrast weak; *ospii* (< *speak*) survives as a verb, the talk-side routes through ideophone+frame. **[Open — register split unresolved; weak diagnostic]**

**Carve decisions:** retain *o* (quotative "say"; perfect *rhentt*), *þilnn* (tell/inform; 3 senses), *ospii* (speak). Ideophones as a new dictionary layer (§4). *talk* — no dedicated verb; routes through ideophone + frame. **[talk/speak Open]**

**Surprises routed onward:** ideophone class + multimodality (§2.5); *-wiis* aboutness marker (§3); the "*a news*" analogical count-noun (§3); *rhishisben* / *kyii* clause linkers (§3); *baurzıĩ* / *belkm(lkm)* greeting senses (§4).

### 1.2 B — Reportative edge

**Clustering verdict:** the dizque-slot prediction **holds**, and resolves into a single general indirect-evidential particle **reś** (< GA *I guess*) that **supersedes *piies***. Personal-source vs general hearsay is carried not by two particles but by **reś + the quotative marker *quo***. The membership criterion is **revised** here (§2.1).

**Findings.**
- **reś, general indirect-evidential matrix particle.** *Reś* covers hearsay and inference alike. Tense-invariant — anchored to the speech "now," and GA *I guess* / *I guessed* converge phonologically, so no surface tense contrast exists (not "past form unattested" — structurally tenseless). *Nou baurrits, reś eos noquii rin riiy* "(You) know the bridge — guess it doesn't quite run." **Supersedes *piies*** (§2.2).
- **Personal source via *quo*.** *Quo* (< GA *quote*) = spoken punctuation marking a personal/quoted source. Position is meaningful: it precedes the salient/topical part of the quote. *Nou baurrits, reś et quo nokw rin riiy* "I heard the bridge is out" (quo before the negation = the not-running is the salient hearsay). Flexible position: after *reś* it scopes the whole clause and the two merge → **resquo** (*Resquo nokw rin riiy*). Position can color speaker doubt (*Reś eia ounaheun quo beussno* "they say they'll turn in — but who knows").
- **Adverb-layer vs particle-layer + reportative-over-*enniip* stacking.** "Apparently she left" = *Reś ei beunheun split*; "I heard she ended up leaving" = *Resquo enniip ounohen splitm oðerren* — reportative stacks over *enniip*, with strong-pronoun *oðerren* buffering (confirms triage §2.2 stacking + B6).
- **Auditory vs visual inference under reś.** Both "sounds like / looks like rain" = *Reś ets ounohen rheinn* or *Reś pons o rheĩ*, **ambiguous as to modality** — mirroring how GA "I see/hear rain" is voice-ambiguous (*rimmase nouśę o rheĩ*). Modality is resolved by **topic-particle choice** (*hii ossii* perceptible / *no ossii* non-perceptible) — i.e. reś **inherits the old *piies* seem/look division of labor** (§2.2). Or by **onomatopoeia introduced by -*ðs*** (< GA *this*): *rimmase nouśnðs riprprp* "noticing-this drip-drip-drip."
- **pons — neutral existential.** *Pons* (< GA *(there is) upon us*) = "there is" (cf. French *il y a*, Spanish *hay*): *pons rheĩ* "there is rain." Irregular diachronic development (§5); a grammatical-word quirk.
- **Inanimate-active-framing carries non-neutral color (new generalization).** Casting weather/inanimates as active (with either frame) is never neutral: go-ahead "it's going ahead and raining (anyway)" = inconvenience/fate; get-oneself "it got itself to rain" = unexpectedness. The grammar **defaults inanimates to impersonal/existential (*pons*)** and reserves active framing for discourse effect. **[Provisional — generalize beyond weather with more data]**
- **Membership criterion revised + *nóuśshè* (§2.1).** "Word is they're hiring" does **not** beget a matrix particle. Mat redefines the class on **syntactic** grounds (clause-scope + fixed clause-initial), retiring the finite-verb-etymon criterion. New member **nóuśshè** (< GA *in a nutshell*; "long story short," cf. Italian *insomma*) — adverbial etymon, no finite verb, yet behaves as a matrix particle. Flexible-position items (*meiyi*, *bauyiis*, *quo*) are thereby **not** matrix particles; discourse adverbials *rhiksse* (< *you could say*), *beiskiiy* (< *basically*), *noê* (< *in a way*) cluster as "in a way / sort of." **[their nuance Open — own session]**

**Carve decisions:** *reś* (supersedes *piies*); *quo* (+ merger *resquo*); *nóuśshè*; *pons*; discourse adverbials *rhiksse / beiskiiy / noê*. **[Settled where marked]**

**Surprises routed onward:** membership-criterion rewrite (§2.1); reś/piies supersession (§2.2); inanimate-framing color (§3); -*ðs* onomatopoeia-object marker (§3); negation reduced form *nokw* + air-quote gesture (§3, §5).

### 1.3 C — Quotation

**Clustering verdict:** the colloquial-quotative prediction **holds** — *o* (< GA *go*) wins; *be all* **dies**. The split-TAM prediction **holds**: the quotative is hosted on the weak pronoun (aspect-on-pronoun), with *o* as the verb. Direct vs indirect is carried by **particle choice**, no complementizer needed.

**Findings.**
- ***o* quotative + optional clarifiers.** *Ei o nóè* "she was like, 'no way'"; with clarifiers *ei o quo o nóè* — *quo* (quotative) + *o* (< GA *oh*, reported-speech opener). The verb *o* is obligatory; *quo* and the *oh*-marker are optional disambiguators. Three homophonous/near-homophonous *o*'s now in play: *o* (< *go*, quotative verb), *o* (< *oh*, quote-opener), and the referential article *o* — disentangle in the registry/dictionary.
- ***be all* does not survive** (quotative). *go* survives (as the core quotative). **[Settled]**
- **Stance-quotation inheritance.** Internal reaction with nothing uttered: *Bauyen, e o quo 'þiiaii'* "I was like, finally!" — *quote-oneself* (quo on one's own thought = internal). The left-dislocated strong pronoun *bauyen* reads as a conversational turn. *Quote-oneself* fails for non-first persons; there, adverbs disambiguate (*eńhii* "inside / internally"; *rhiþoupii* < *without a peep*). Overlaps COGNITION quotable-thought residue.
- **Direct vs indirect by particle.** Indirect: *eia quo ounohen meiknet* "she said she'd come" (the form alone suffices for indirect). Direct: *bentt oðerren quo, o mau ouhen meiktt* "she was like, 'I'll come'" — clause-initial *quo* + *oh*-opener mark the whole as a direct quote. Confirms the prediction: distinction is entirely particle choice.

**Carve decisions:** *o* (quotative verb, retained from §1.1); *quo* (quotative particle, retained from §1.2); *o* (< *oh*) quote-opener; *be all* not lexified. **[Settled]**

**Surprises routed onward:** the three-*o* homophony tangle (§3); *quote-oneself* internal-thought strategy (§3, COGNITION cross-ref); strong-pronoun left-dislocation in turn-taking (§3).

### 1.4 G — Interactive / durative (partial)

**Clustering verdict:** activities run on the get-oneself frame + ideophone, aspect-spread as predicted. **Reciprocity is unmarked** on the we-subject (first data point). REC/ITER nominals **not tested** — deprecated (§2.3). Stimulus-promotion **generalizes** outside statives.

**Findings.**
- **Aspect spread on the chat stem.** *Rhii riauset rekriiâkm ą piem hii* "we were chatting all evening" (imperfect, ideophone *rekriiâ* verbalized with concord *-km*); *rhii riauseto rekriiâ* "we got to chatting" (get-oneself + onset). Note **ideophones verbalize** — take frame + concord (*rekriiâkm*). Ties to §2.5.
- **Reciprocity unmarked.** "We argued about money" = *riauseto bekw noqoų ba'ųıì* — we-subject get-oneself carries reciprocity with no dedicated marker. **[Provisional — first data for a reciprocal section]**
- **Stimulus-promotion outside statives.** "Money got us arguing" = *riusto bekw, o ba'ųıì* — the stimulus-promotion strategy (round 1, statives) **generalizes** to activities. Strengthens triage §1.6's strategy-generality.
- **REC/ITER not tested — deprecated (§2.3).** "The chats" skipped. Battery B1 **not run** (cancelled, §6).

**Carve decisions:** *bekw* (< *bicker*; argue), *rekriiâ* as verbalizable ideophone. **[Settled]** *gossip*, *discuss* untested. **[Open — round 3]**

**Surprises routed onward:** unmarked reciprocity (§3); ideophone verbalization (§2.5); *-wiis*/*ą … hii* adverbial framing (§3).

### 1.5 E — Speech acts (partial)

**Clustering verdict:** performatives are strongly volitive; the **commitment-trap** reading (get-oneself on a performative = reluctance/social pressure) **holds**. Formulaic acts (*thank/apologize/greet*) **interjection-ize** as predicted. *ask* = *rhinnw*, frame-split in meaning.

**Findings.**
- **Commitment trap.** "I promised" = *Beunheun beŕaumss* (volitive); "I ended up promising" = *Enniip ounohen beŕaumssm ą bauyen* — *enniip* + get-oneself = reluctant/pressured commitment. Sharpened DOING-attitudinal recurrence.
- **ask = rhinnw, frame-conditioned sense.** *Rhinnw* (< GA *wonder*) shifts to "ask/inquire" under **get-oneself**, but "doubt / be skeptical" under **go-ahead**. *Ritmseto rhinnw, emii ounohen meiknet ea* "she asked if I was coming" — note **inverted *emii* + *ea*** for conditional ("whether"), formally identical to the interrogative (*am I going to make it at all?*), distinguished only by rising tone. Direct question: *ei o, ohei meikntt ea* — interrogative quote-marker **ohei** (< GA *oh hey*) + clipped question (clipping licensed in questions, cf. *bǫëiio ea* "(want) coffee?").
- **Interjection-ization.** "I apologized to her" = *beunheun pauyiist·wm* (verb *pauyiist*); "I was like, my bad" = *ei o quo pauyiiś* (interjection *pauyiiś* < *apologies*, via the quotative workaround). Confirms formulaic-act → interjection.

**Carve decisions:** *beŕaumss* (promise), *rhinnw* (ask/doubt, frame-split), *pauyiist* (apologize, v.) + *pauyiiś* (interj.), *ohei* (direct-Q marker). *thank/warn/greet* untested. **[Open — round 3]**

**Surprises routed onward:** *rhinnw* frame-conditioned sense (a sense-split parallel to *nouś/not* tendencies — §3); *emii … ea* conditional = interrogative-homophone (§3, glossing); clipped questions (§3).

### 1.6 MOTION make-up (M2–M4; M5 skipped)

**Clustering verdict:** deixis is handled **lexically** (frozen deictic adverbs), not by speaker-anchored grammar; caused-motion deixis reuses the get-it frame. M5 (the walks REC nominal) **skipped** — deprecated (§2.3).

**Findings.**
- **come/go deixis — lexical.** "Come here" = *reshiseto rheiiem rhilqui* (get-oneself + *rheiiem* < *where I am* = "here" + *rhilqui* < *real quick*, command softener); alt. interjection *meirei* (< *m'aidez*) "come!" (addressee-unspecified). "Go there" = *reshise ouðeu* (*ouðeu* < *over there* = "there"). Deixis lives in the frozen adverbs *rheiiem / ouðeu*, not in the verb.
- **durative vs onset (re-test).** "We were driving for hours" = *riausse rhiimm þri·ei·iiǫ* (imperfect durative); "we set off at dawn" = *rennausseto split him eiem* (onset, infinitive-shaped; *him eiem* "come A.M."). Onset = imperfect-tied inchoative machinery, as re-predicted.
- **caused-motion deixis = get-it frame.** "Bring it to me" = *reuto mii*; "take it there" = *reut ouðeu*. Bring/take = get-it + deictic adverb (no separate bring/take verbs; the possession-causative *reut*/*reuto* + deixis covers it).

**Carve decisions:** deictic adverbs *rheiiem* ("here"), *ouðeu* ("there"); *meirei* (interj. "come"); *rhilqui* (command softener); *reut(o)* (bring/take = get-it caused motion). **[Settled]** Deixis question from round 1 (go≠come?) — **resolved lexically**.

## 2. Decisions from the 2026-06-18 discussion session

### 2.1 Matrix-particle membership criterion — revised **[Settled 2026-06-18]**

**Decision.** Membership in the matrix-particle class is defined by **two syntactic diagnostics**: (1) **clause-scope** (the particle colors a whole clause), and (2) **fixed clause-initial position**. The round-1 criterion — "the etymon contains a finite verb (→ tense-capable)" — is **retired as criterial** and demoted to an **origin correlate**: it explains why *some* members (*enp/enniip*, *no/nii*) carry tense, but it does not define the class.

**Consequences.**
- **Name kept.** "Matrix particle" stays (the prototype is still matrix-clause-derived; renaming churns the registry + three docs for little gain). The matrix-clause origin moves wholly into the Origin subsection.
- **Tense demoted.** The §11 headline "tense survives only here" is reframed: tense is a **sub-property some members inherit from a finite-verb etymon**, not a class trait. Tenseless members (*reś*, *nóuśshè*, the retired *piies*) are members in good standing.
- **New member *nóuśshè*** (< GA *in a nutshell*; paraphrase/summary "long story short"; cf. Italian *insomma*) — adverbial etymon, no finite verb, fixed clause-initial by GA phrasal habit. Direct vindication of the syntactic criterion.
- **Non-members reclassified on syntactic grounds.** *meiyi*, *bauyiis*, *quo* take flexible position (*meiyi ei beunheun split* whole-clause vs *ei beunheun meiyi split* part-modifying) → modal adverbs / flexible particles, not matrix particles. (Previously excluded for "no finite verb"; now excluded for flexible position — the new reasoning supersedes the old.)

**This resolves** the round-1 registry framing of the class and rewrites `verbal-system.md` §11.5 (Origin) and §11 (the architecture lead).

### 2.2 *reś* supersedes *piies* — full supersession **[Settled 2026-06-18]**

**Decision.** **reś** (< GA *I guess*) is the **general indirect-evidential matrix particle** (hearsay + inference). It is structurally **tenseless** (speech-anchored; *I guess/guessed* converge). It **fully supersedes *piies*** (< *it appears*), which is **retired**. The seem/look **division of labor** (perceptible vs non-perceptible) that *piies* carried is **absorbed by reś**, with the contrast now borne by **topic-particle choice** (*hii ossii* perceptible / *no ossii* non-perceptible) — and, for fine modality, by onomatopoeia under -*ðs*.

**This resolves** `verbal-system.md` §11.7's "*piies* past form" open question (by deletion of the member). Keep a supersession trail per house style (§11.5 / Versioning Notes; registry History). Propagates to the round-1 seem/look finding in `language_reference.md` §3.7 (*piies* → *reś*).

### 2.3 REC/ITER habitual nominal — demotion confirmed and completed **[Settled 2026-06-18]**

**Decision.** Round-1 triage §2.1 already demoted the recognitional-iterative (shared-knowledge habitual) nominal out of the verbal system (special case of the possession construction). Round 2 **confirms and completes** the demotion: the habitual *construction* is **no longer a verbal-system feature and is no longer a test target** (items 13c "the chats," 15, M5 "the walks," and battery B1 are **cancelled** — §6). What survives is the **fossilized/lexicalized definite article** (the *þ/o* doublet), now treated purely as **nominal/determiner morphology**.

**Relocation (updated).** `verbal-system.md` §7 reduces to a brief demotion pointer (or is removed, replaced by a Versioning note); the *þ/o* determiner doublet and its grammaticalization story move to the nominal/determiner-morphology material in `language_reference.md`. The round-1 reasoning (the flagship-innovation framing, the Latin *ille* parallel) is **preserved in a quarantine container** (Origin subsection + Versioning Notes), per house style — the supersession is traceable, not erased. Batteries B1/B8 are struck (§6).

### 2.4 Malefactive get-oneself reading **[Provisional 2026-06-18]**

**Decision.** The get-oneself frame's auto-benefactive can flip **malefactive** in a zero-sum reading: another party's benefit registers as the speaker's detriment. *Rimmaśe oę rhémmen·bèm, rhishisben ei retmseto baurzıĩ* "…when she barged in" — get-oneself on *baurzıĩ* gives the intrusion a mildly undesirable color (the neutral alternative uses go-ahead: *… rhishisben ei ouheun baurzıĩ*). New documented reading; single attestation. Route to `verbal-system.md` §4.2 (frame semantics) as **[Provisional]** — collect more cases before Settled.

### 2.5 The ideophone / multimodal layer **[class Settled 2026-06-18; home → new sibling `ideophones.md`, ratified 2026-06-20]**

**Decision.** Ideophones are a **productive class characterizing speech acts** (and, by extension, other events), **obligatory** after the quotative verb *o*. Properties:
- **Phonotactically marginal:** may contain non-phonemes or rare phonemes and may bear ultimate-syllable stress, breaking constraints binding on the core lexicon.
- **Verbalizable:** take frame + concord (*rekriiâkm* "chatting").
- **Multimodal:** several carry conventionalized **hand gestures** (*rekriiâ* / *bauwouwou* = wrist-rotation "and so on"; *rhémm·bèm* = back-of-hand rhythmic clap). Documents the multimodality of everyday conversation.
- **Attested members:** *rhémmen·bèm* / *rhémm·bèm* (bullet-point/confrontational), *rekriiâ* (dialect speech; **overrides existing *rékriiyæ*** — §2.6), *rhitmmtm* (negation/refusal; *t* → glottal stop; stress on geminate syllabic *m*), *bauwouwou* (speaking GA), plus onomatopoeia *riprprp* (drip), *bet bät* (pitter-patter) introduced by -*ðs*.

**Home — ratified 2026-06-20.** The canonical home is a **new sibling deep-dive, `ideophones.md`**, parallel to `verbal-system.md`, on a hub-and-spoke model:
- **Why a sibling, not a subsection or appendix.** It is a genuinely new descriptive *domain* — not a development notebook folded in (cf. the verb-recipe appendix principle), and not derivative of an existing sibling. It passes the genre test the project uses for siblings. Crucially it introduces an apparatus **no existing doc can host**: conventionalized co-speech **gesture / multimodality notation**. And it is already slated as a **first-class round-3 target** (§7.2), so it will accrete; a thin sibling is fine under the assembler model (the PDF build is Part-per-doc; the site inlines), and standing it up now avoids a later extract-and-retrofit out of `language_reference.md`.
- **Hub (`ideophones.md`):** class definition; properties (phonotactic marginality, verbalizability with frame + concord, obligatoriness after quotative *o*); the gesture/multimodal notation convention; member inventory with sketches and `[Settled]/[Provisional]/[Open]` tags.
- **Spokes (each canonical where it already lives, pointing back to the hub):** phonology (the phonotactic-exemption rule, §5); orthography (interpunct *·*, glottal-stop *t*, -*ðs*); dictionary (D4-B members, tagged to the class); `verbal-system.md` Quotation § (quotative-*o* obligatoriness); `language_reference.md` (assembler stub + orientation abstract per the assembly model).
- **Deferred:** a `poetics.md` sound-symbolism cross-ref — only if/when that doc is created; do not stand `poetics.md` up now for this.

This becomes **Session I** in §8. The registry (Session T) still adds the **ideophone** class term regardless of how ratification lands.

**Decision.** The existing dictionary entry *rékriiyæ* is **overridden in form** by *rekriiâ* and **gains the ideophone sense** (dialect/"Rickety Yak" speech). One entry, updated — not a parallel headword. Route to the dictionary session (§4); flag the form change in the dictionary changelog.
