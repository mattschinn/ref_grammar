# Progressive (‑ing) & Preterite (‑ed) Coda Survey — Battery B12

**Status:** working analysis / draft. Feeds `phonology.md` (morphophonology) and `verbal-system.md` §4.2 (concord shapes). Predictions here are **hypotheses for elicitation**, not committed rules — the §3.22 finding is **[Open]**.
**Date:** 2026‑06‑22. **Scope:** every verb lemma in `dictionary.md` plus the SAYING‑round verbs (triage §4 D4‑A), sorted by **conlang surface coda**, with the GA source coda alongside and a predicted ‑ing / ‑ed reflex for each class.

---

## 1. The two increments

Two pieces of inflectional morphology are at issue. Both are **concord shapes** in the sense of `verbal-system.md` §4.2 — endings the main verb carries in concord with its frame — and both are diachronic reflexes of GA verbal suffixes.

**PROG — the progressive‑shaped concord.** Reflex of GA *‑ing* /‑ɪŋ/ → /‑ɪn/ (the colloquial *‑in'* pronunciation; cf. the *‑in* of placeholder *relaxin*, §4.2). Synchronically it behaves as a **nasal increment** that docks onto the stem coda. It is the *shape* exemplified by *beussno*, *rheinn*, *rhiimm*, *rhikriutm*, *bekrrıĩ* (§3.22) and by attested *splitm* "leaving."

**PRET — the preterite / perfect / passive concord (the "‑ed" family).** Reflex of GA *‑ed* /‑d ~ ‑t/ and the strong preterite, surfacing as a **coronal‑stop increment** /‑T/: plain *‑t* (*pauyiist*), geminate *‑tt*, glottal *‑ʔ* in isolation, or *‑tm* when further suffixes follow (*þiltm·ę·aų·et*). The regular exponent is the *‑t(t)‑* infix of §4.2.1; irregulars (strong verbs) keep GA morphology (*meiktt* → concord *meirtt*).

**The unifying observation.** Both increments routinely **resurrect an etymological coda that the regular sound‑change cascade deleted or altered.** GA codas that vanish word‑finally (*recruit* /t/ → *rhikriu*, *click* /k/ → *kli*) or mutate (*drive* /v/ → *rhiib* /b/, *bicker* /r/ → *bekw* /w̩/) reappear under inflection (*rhikriutm*, *rhiimm*, *bekrrıĩ*). The inflected stem is partly a **diachronic fossil**: the suffix re‑exposes the underlying form. This is why coda shape is so strongly predictive — and why the bare lemma sometimes *under*‑determines the inflected form (two lemmas that fell together in the citation form can split again under ‑ing; see §4, Class V).

A second principle, already on record: **coda material resurfaces intervocalically under vowel‑initial concord** (`verbal-system.md` §4.2.2, *bemmorret* ~ *bemmorreųt*; the rhotic of GA *remember* returns between vowels). PROG and PRET both trigger this.

## 3. How to read the coda columns

- **GA coda** = the consonant(s) closing the source word (or the source *phrase*'s final word, for fused phrasals like *pass out*, *pick up*). "(→∅)" marks a GA coda that the cascade deletes word‑finally; "(→X)" a coda that mutates.
- **Conlang coda** = the surface coda of the citation form, from the dictionary IPA.
- Classes are ordered roughly by how much the coda *resists* vs. *hosts* the nasal increment.

## 4. Verbs by conlang coda shape, with predictions

### Class V — oral vowel‑final
**Members:** *o* (*go*), *ospii* (*speak*), *epplii* (*apply*), *kli* (*click*), *spyi* (*spew*), *piquô* (*pick out*), *piiquóä* (*peek over at*), *rhiitsô* (*reach out*), *rhiiynii* (*really need*), *rhiiysseunnou* (*really stand out*), *rékriiyä* (*yakitty‑yak*), *noë* (*annoy*), *beusso/beussou* (*pass out*), *rhikriu* (*recruit*), *reuto* (*get it*).

This is the largest and **least uniform** class, because a surface vowel‑final lemma hides one of two histories:

- **(a) Genuinely vowel‑final in GA** (*apply* /aɪ/, *spew* /uː/, *annoy* /ɔɪ/): nothing to resurrect. **PROG predicted: nasal infixes / appends before or onto the final vowel**, on the *beussno* model. *epplii* → *epplinni*; *spyi* → *spinni* ~ *spyinni*; *noë* → *noenn*. **PRET predicted: bare /‑t/ on the vowel** — *eppliit*, *noët*.
- **(b) Vowel‑final only by GA‑coda deletion** (*recruit* /t/, *click* /k/, *speak* /k/, *pick out* /t/, *reach out* /t/, *get it* /t/): the **suppressed GA stop resurfaces** under inflection, exactly as in *rhikriutm*. **PROG predicted: /‑Tm̩/** — *kli* → *klikm*, *ospii* → *ospikm*, *piquô* → *piquotm*, *rhiitsô* → *rhiitsotm*. **PRET predicted: the stop + /t/** (or a geminate) — *kli* → *klikt*, *ospii* → *ospikt*.

> **This is the single most important prediction of the survey:** for Class V, the *citation form does not tell you the inflected form.* The author should elicit ‑ing on a vowel‑final lemma of *known* GA‑stop origin (*click*, *speak*) to confirm the resurrection pattern, and on a true‑vowel‑final lemma (*apply*, *spew*) to confirm the bare‑nasal‑infix pattern. If both hold, PROG/PRET become a **diagnostic for the etymological coda** — a clean internal‑reconstruction tool.

### Class Ṽ — nasal‑vowel‑final
**Members:** *rheĩ* (*rain*), *rhıĩ* (*ring*), *ǫ* (*own*), *bényǫ* (*opinion*), *baurzıĩ* (*barge in*).
**Source codas:** GA /n/, /ŋ/ — nasality already lives on the vowel.
**PROG (attested on *rheĩ→rheinn*):** the latent nasal **geminates / surfaces as a coda nasal** → /‑Ṽnː/. *rhıĩ* → *rhıĩnn*; *ǫ* → *onn*; *bényǫ* → *bényonn*; *baurzıĩ* → *baurzıĩnn*.
**PRET predicted:** nasal vowel + /t/ → /‑Ṽt/ — *rheĩ* → *rheĩt*, *ǫ* → *ǫt*. (Possible nasal‑t cluster /‑nt/ if the nasal hardens; elicit.)

### Class Db — voiced‑stop‑final (all surface /b/)
**Members:** *rhiib* (*drive*, GA /v/), *rib* (*give*, GA /v/), *preb* (*prep*, GA /p/).
**PROG (attested on *rhiib→rhiimm*):** the stop **assimilates to a homorganic nasal geminate** → labial /b/ → /mː/. *rib* → *rimm*; *preb* → *premm*. (Predicted general rule: /b d g/ → /mː nː ŋː/ under PROG; only labials attested so far.)
**PRET predicted:** stop devoices and clusters with /t/ → /‑pt/ — *rhiib* → *rhiipt*, *rib* → *ript*, *preb* → *prept*. Whether it is /‑pt/ or a glottalized geminate */‑pp/* is open.

### Class Tp — voiceless‑stop‑final
**Members:** *split* (*split*, /t/), *pot* (*pout*, /t/), *reut* (*get it*, /t/), *rérèyt* (*narrate*, /t/), *bęiink* (*panic*, /k/).
**PROG (attested on *split→splitm*):** stop + **syllabic nasal** → /‑Tm̩/ (identical surface result to the Class‑V(b) resurrection, since the stop is already present). *pot* → *potm*; *reut* → *reutm*; *rérèyt* → *rérèytm*; *bęiink* → *bęiinkm*.
**PRET predicted:** the coda already *is* a coronal stop, so PRET should show **gemination or glottalization rather than an added segment** — *split* → *splitt* ~ *spliʔ*, *pot* → *pott*. For the velar *bęiink*, a true added /t/ → *bęiinkt*.

### Class Glt — glottal‑stop‑final
**Members:** *nott* (*note*, /t/), *meiktt* (*make it*, /t/ → /kʔ/).
**Note:** these are Class‑Tp stops already weakened to /ʔ/ (E2a). The lemma's glottal *is* an eroded coronal.
**PROG predicted:** glottal restitutes to a stop + syllabic nasal → *nott* → *notm*, *meiktt* → *meiktm*.
**PRET:** for *nott* the glottal already encodes the historical /‑t/, so PRET is likely **vacuous in isolation** (*nott*) and only re‑exposes /t/ under agglutination (cf. *þiltm*). *meiktt* is **irregular** → *meirtt* (§4.2.1).

### Class S — /s/‑final (incl. geminate /sː/)
**Members:** *nouś* (*notice*), *rhemmêś* (*reminisce*), *pauyiis* (*apologize*); geminates *baurrâuss* (*process*), *beŕaumss* (*promise*), and the cluster *þokss* (*focus*, /ksː/).
**PRET (attested on *pauyiis→pauyiist*):** /s/ + t → /‑st/. *nouś* → *nouśt*; *rhemmêś* → *rhemmêśt*; *baurrâuss* → *baurrâusst*; *beŕaumss* → *beŕaumsst*.
**PROG predicted:** a sibilant blocks nasal assimilation, so the nasal should surface **syllabic** → /‑sn̩/. *nouś* → *nouśn*; *rhemmêś* → *rhemmêśn*; *pauyiis* → *pauyiisn*. (Alternatively a buffer vowel: /‑sən/. Elicit.)

### Class Sh — /ʃ/‑final
**Members:** *ŕeush* (*cherish*), *rhiiyshêush* (*really cherish*).
**PROG predicted:** syllabic nasal, as Class S → *ŕeushn*.
**PRET predicted:** /ʃ/ + t → /‑ʃt/ → *ŕeusht*, *rhiiyshêusht*.

### Class W — syllabic /w̩/ appendix (vocalized GA *‑er* / *‑le*)
**Members:** from GA **‑er**: *bemmw* (*remember*), *bekw* (*bicker*), *rhinnw* (*wonder*), *baunnw* (*ponder*); from GA **‑le**: *barnkw* (*barnacle*), *toutw* (*total to*); other: *ŕequôþw* (*check off*).
This class behaves specially because the /w̩/ is a **vocalized rhotic or lateral**, and inflection brings the original liquid back (the §4.2.2 resurfacing).
**PROG (attested on *bekw→bekrrıĩ*; ‑er source):** **linking‑r returns** + nasal vowel → /‑rːı̃/. *bemmw* → *bemrrıĩ*; *rhinnw* → *rhinrrıĩ*; *baunnw* → *baunrrıĩ*.
**PRET (attested on *bemmw→bemmorret*; ‑er source):** rhotic resurfaces **intervocalically** + /t/ → /‑orret/. *bekw* → *bekorret*; *rhinnw* → *rhinorret*.
**Open — the ‑le subclass.** *barnacle*/*total* gave /w̩/ from a **lateral**, not a rhotic. Does the lateral come back (*barnkw* → *barnklı̃*?), or does it merge into the ‑er pattern (rhotacized *barnkrrıĩ*)? **No data — elicit a ‑le verb directly** (suggestions in §5).

### Class N — syllabic geminate nasal
**Member:** *þilnn* (*fill in*).
**PROG predicted:** already maximally nasal; the increment should be **absorbed** (length/no change) — *þilnn*.
**PRET (attested, agglutinating):** glottal‑t → /‑tm/ before further suffixes (*þiltm·ę·aų·et*).

### Class App / Clu — stop appendix & complex clusters
**Members:** *pikkp* (*pick up*, /‑p/), *bessts* (*message*, /sːts/), *rhiušp* (*worship*, /ʃp/), *ospokwþ* (*speak with*, /kwθ/).
Heavy edge cases; the increment is forced syllabic. **PROG:** *pikkp* → *pikkpm*; *rhiušp* → *rhiušpm*. **PRET:** *pikkp* → *pikkpt*; cluster behavior otherwise open. Flag for individual treatment, not a general rule.

## 5. Coda shapes that are missing or thin — and verbs to fill them

Two kinds of gap. **Principled gaps** (the cascade actively bans the shape) and **survey holes** (the shape is licit but unattested for verbs). Suggested test verbs give the GA form and the prediction the elicitation would check.

### Principled gaps (confirm the ban, don't expect the coda)
- **Voiced‑fricative codas /z v ð/** — *none*, and apparently by rule: GA */z/* devoices (*apologize* → *pauyiis* /s/) and GA */v/* stops (*drive*, *give* → /b/). **Test:** *use* /juːz/, *please* /pliːz/, *breathe* /briːð/. Prediction: /‑s/ (and a Class‑S inflection) or /‑b/. If any voiced fricative *survives*, that's a real finding for `phonology.md` §4.4.
- **Lateral /l/ coda** — *none*; GA dark /ɫ/ vocalizes everywhere (§4.4.4). Not a verb‑specific hole.

### Survey holes (licit codas, no verb yet) — **recommended elicitation set**
1. **Rhotic coda /ɾ/** (from back‑vowel dark‑l, §4.4.4 → /ɻ/ → /ɾ/): GA *pull* /pʊɫ/, *call* /kɔɫ/. Prediction: *pull* → */pʊɾ/* with a **surface tap coda** — the only route to a non‑vocalized rhotic. Tests whether PROG rhotacizes further or nasalizes (*puɾm*? *purrıĩ*?).
2. **Glide /j/ coda** (from front‑vowel dark‑l, §4.4.4): GA *feel* /fiːɫ/, *deal*, *heal*. Prediction: */‑ij/* coda. Tests PROG on a glide (geminate *‑iyy*? nasal infix *‑ijn*?).
3. **Consonantal nasal coda /m n/** (vs. the Class‑Ṽ nasal‑vowel outcome): GA *seem* /siːm/, *blame* /bleɪm/, *learn* /lɝn/. Prediction: does the nasal stay a **coda consonant** or colour the vowel (→ Class Ṽ)? This directly tests the boundary of Class Ṽ.
4. **Affricate coda /tʃ dʒ/**: GA *teach* /tiːtʃ/, *watch* /wɑtʃ/, *judge* /dʒʌdʒ/. Prediction: /tʃ/ → /ts/ (cf. *reach out* → *rhiitsô* medially) giving a **/‑ts/ coda**; PROG then like Class Tp (*‑tsm̩*?).
5. **Bare voiceless non‑sibilant fricative /f θ/**: GA *laugh* /læf/, *cough* /kɔf/, *bathe→breath* /θ/. Prediction: survives as /‑f/, /‑θ/ (cf. *ospokwþ*, *ŕequôþw* where /θ/ persists in cluster); PROG syllabic (*‑fm̩*, *‑θm̩*) like Class S.
6. **Velar nasal /ŋ/ — confirm the merger.** GA *‑ing*‑stem or *bang*, *hang*. Prediction: → nasal vowel (Class Ṽ), as *ring* → *rhıĩ*. Likely already resolved; one datapoint would close it.

A compact elicitation battery, then, is: **pull, feel, seem, teach, laugh, use** (six verbs) — one per hole, each chosen so its GA coda is unambiguous.

## 6. Summary of predicted morphophonology

| Conlang coda class | PROG (‑ing, nasal /‑N/) | PRET (‑ed, coronal /‑T/) | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ṽ nasal‑vowel | nasal geminates /‑Ṽnː/ | /‑Ṽt/ | PROG **attested** |
| V — true vowel | nasal infix /‑nV/ | /‑Vt/ | PROG **attested** (*beussno*) |
| V — masks GA stop | stop resurfaces + /‑m̩/ → /‑Tm̩/ | stop + /t/ | PROG **attested** (*rhikriutm*) |
| Db voiced stop | nasalizes → geminate nasal /‑Nː/ | devoice + /t/ → /‑pt/ | PROG **attested** (*rhiimm*) |
| Tp voiceless stop | /‑Tm̩/ syllabic nasal | geminate/glottal /‑tt ~ ʔ/ | PROG **attested** (*splitm*) |
| Glt glottal | restitute stop + /‑m̩/ | vacuous / agglutinating /‑tm/ | predicted |
| S sibilant /s sː/ | syllabic /‑sn̩/ | /‑st/ | PRET **attested** (*pauyiist*) |
| Sh /ʃ/ | syllabic /‑ʃn̩/ | /‑ʃt/ | predicted |
| W /w̩/ (‑er) | linking‑r + nasal V /‑rːı̃/ | rhotic + /t/ /‑orret/ | both **attested** |
| W /w̩/ (‑le) | ? lateral return vs. rhotacism | ? | **gap — elicit** |
| N syll. nasal | absorbed (no change) | agglutinating /‑tm/ | PRET **attested** (*þiltm*) |
| App / Clu | syllabic /‑m̩/ | + /t/ | per‑item, open |

**Two rules worth proposing to `phonology.md` once confirmed:**
1. **PROG = a nasal mora** that (i) geminates a nasal coda, (ii) nasalizes a voiced stop, (iii) surfaces as a syllabic nasal after a voiceless stop or sibilant, (iv) infixes before a final vowel, and (v) triggers liquid‑resurrection in the /w̩/ class. The single morpheme, five contextual reflexes.
2. **Inflection re‑exposes etymological codas** deleted word‑finally by the cascade — making PROG/PRET a synchronic window onto the GA source coda (the "fossil" effect).
